Who I Am

I'm a Cisco-certified Network Engineer with 6.3 years of hands-on experience in networking and network security. I specialize in configuring and troubleshooting L2/L3 networks, with deep expertise across Juniper, Cisco, and Arista platforms — and firewall experience spanning Palo Alto, FortiGate, and Checkpoint.

Currently at Terralogic Software Solutions, leading a team of six engineers managing production SP and data-center infrastructure. I'm on a deliberate 18-month path toward a Network + Security + Cloud engineering role — deepening SP/DC protocol knowledge first, then layering in security engineering and cloud-native skills.
