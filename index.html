About

Who I Am

I'm a Senior Network Engineer at Viasat, working on the Frontline team of a global network engineering organization. My day-to-day covers device operations, upgrades, alert handling, and configuration changes across Juniper, Cisco, and Arista equipment at service-provider and data-center scale.

Before Viasat, I worked across banking and healthcare B2B clients — building network ops, troubleshooting, and firewall exposure with Checkpoint and Fortinet. I hold a CCNA (2020) and am actively deepening my expertise in SP protocols, DC fabrics, and network security engineering.

Beyond ops, I ship internal tooling: Python + Streamlit dashboards that turn raw operational data (Jira, Confluence, NetBox, SSH) into actionable visibility for my team.
