About

Who I Am

I'm a Senior Network Engineer currently at Terralogic Software Solutions, leading a team of six engineers on global SP and data-center infrastructure. My work spans VXLAN/EVPN overlay design, BGP/OSPF troubleshooting, CGNAT, OOB management, Splunk-driven monitoring, and FortiGate firewall operations — with direct Vendor TAC engagement across Cisco, Juniper, and Arista.

Before that, I've worked across Bahrain (Epsilon InfoTech), Bangalore (FIS Global, Ahalia Group), gaining hands-on experience in Checkpoint firewall HA clusters, DC-to-DR switchovers for a global financial corporation, and FortiGate SD-WAN in healthcare environments.

Beyond ops, I build internal tooling — Python and Streamlit dashboards that pull from Jira, Confluence, NetBox, and raw SSH output to give my team real-time operational visibility. I also integrate AI workflows and MCP-based tool automation into my daily engineering practice.

I'm on a deliberate 18-month path toward Network + Security + Cloud — deepening SP/DC protocol knowledge first, then layering security engineering and cloud-native skills on top.
